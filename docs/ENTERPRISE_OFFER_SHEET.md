## ATLAS in one sentence

**ATLAS OS turns a building from a handover event into a live, measurable operating asset on day one.**

## What ATLAS delivers

ATLAS OS provides a live operating layer across buildings and portfolios:

- digital twin visibility for operators and leadership
- per-floor telemetry and incident triage
- resident broadcast with operational record
- fleet-level visibility across multiple assets
- auditable energy, water, air, and resilience metrics

ATLAS does **not** replace safety-rated controls. AI advises and surfaces risk; deterministic controls and human operators retain life-safety authority.

## Who it is for

### Developers and owners

Use ATLAS to reduce commissioning friction, create earlier operating visibility, and hand over a building with a live operating system instead of static manuals and disconnected data.

### Portfolio operators

Use ATLAS to standardize visibility across buildings, improve incident awareness, and give asset, operations, and sustainability teams one shared performance layer.

### Cities and public housing groups

Use ATLAS to make resilience measurable and accountable across housing programs, mixed-use developments, or public-private pilots.
